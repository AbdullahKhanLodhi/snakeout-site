Snake Out — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 September 2026 · Android

Snake Out ("the app") is published by kidopoint ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app handles, why, and the choices you have. It applies to the Android version of Snake Out distributed through Google Play.

  • Snake Out has no accounts, no sign-in, and no servers of our own. We never see your name, email, contacts, photos, or precise location.
  • Your game progress, coins, best times, and settings are stored only on your device.
  • The app shows ads through Google AdMob. Google's advertising SDK may collect device identifiers and diagnostic data to serve and measure ads. This is the only personal data that leaves your device, and it goes to Google, not to us.
  • The app is not directed at children under 13.

1. Information stored on your device (never sent to us)

The app saves the following locally using Android's app storage so your game picks up where you left off:

  • Levels completed, current level, and best times
  • Coins earned and spent, hint/undo usage
  • Daily streak dates
  • Settings: sound, haptics, "show ready snakes", reduce motion
  • Whether the one-time tutorial has been shown

This data never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app deletes it. We have no way to read it.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Snake Out is free and supported by ads served by Google AdMob, part of Google LLC. Depending on your device settings and where you live, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • Your device's advertising ID (resettable in Android settings)
  • IP address, from which a coarse, city-level location may be inferred
  • Device and app information: model, OS version, language, screen size, app package name and version
  • Ad interaction data: which ads were shown, viewed, or tapped, and crash or performance diagnostics related to ad delivery

Google uses this to show ads, measure their performance, prevent fraud, and, where you have consented, to personalise the ads you see. How Google uses this data is described in Google's policy for partner sites and apps and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices about ads

  • Personalised vs. non-personalised ads. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, and certain US states, the app shows a consent form before the first ad. You can decline personalised ads and the app will still work; you will see non-personalised ads instead. You can change your choice later from the app's Profile screen.
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in Android: Settings → Google → Ads (wording varies by device). Deleting the ID stops it from being used for personalisation.
  • Rewarded ads (for example, "+1 heart" or "×2 coins") are always optional. Nothing in the game requires you to watch one.

We do not currently offer an in-app purchase to remove ads. If we add one, this policy will be updated.

3. Information we do not collect

We do not collect, and the app does not request permission for: your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or files, camera, microphone, precise (GPS) location, health data, or payment information. The app requests only the INTERNET permission, which is needed to load ads.

4. Crash and performance diagnostics

If you experience a crash, Google Play may offer to send an anonymised crash report to us through the Play Console. That report contains technical details (device model, OS version, stack trace) and no personal information. We may in future add a crash-reporting library (such as Firebase Crashlytics); if we do, this section will be updated before the change ships.

5. Children

Snake Out is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed at children under 13 (or under the age of digital consent in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the app's ads, contact us at kidopoint.official@gmail.com and we will work with Google to have it removed.

6. Data retention

  • Local game data stays on your device until you clear the app's storage or uninstall it.
  • Data processed by Google for advertising is retained according to Google's retention policies, which we do not control.

7. Security

Local game data is protected by Android's per-app sandbox. All communication with Google's ad servers uses HTTPS.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR in Europe or the CCPA/CPRA in California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of your personal data, and to object to or withdraw consent for personalised advertising. Because we hold no personal data ourselves, the practical ways to exercise these rights are:

  • withdrawing ad-personalisation consent in the app (Profile → Privacy options), and
  • resetting or deleting your advertising ID in Android settings.

For anything else, email kidopoint.official@gmail.com; we will respond within 30 days.

9. International transfers

Ad data processed by Google may be transferred to and stored on servers outside your country, including in the United States, under Google's data transfer safeguards.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. The effective date at the top will change, and material changes will be noted in the app's Play Store release notes. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.

11. Contact

Questions or requests: kidopoint.official@gmail.com
